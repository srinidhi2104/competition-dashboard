⚠️ VERIFIED SITUATION (January 2026)

Venezuela's oil production collapsed from 3.5 million bbl/day in 1998 to 896,000 bbl/day in December 2025 (OPEC data). This represents less than 1% of global production despite holding the world's largest proven oil reserves at 303 billion barrels.

Critical Context: On January 3, 2026, U.S. military forces captured President Nicolás Maduro. Vice President Delcy Rodríguez now serves as interim president. This fundamentally changes the investment landscape.
